# Cambrian Bio

> Cambrian Bio (Cambrian BioPharma, Inc.) is a clinical-stage drug development company
> working on the biology of aging. It sources promising early-stage science and advances
> each program through a separate subsidiary ("PipeCo") under a shared development engine,
> targeting the metabolic pathways that decline with age — first as treatments for chronic
> metabolic disease, ultimately as preventive medicines that extend healthspan. Its lead
> program, ATX-304, is an AMPK network activator.
